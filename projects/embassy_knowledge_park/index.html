The Project

A new perspective on integrated living.

Embassy Knowledge Park is a premium integrated residential township development by Embassy Group, located at Tharahunise, Chapparkallu Road, off IVC Road, North Bengaluru.

Spread across approximately 200+ acres, the development is planned to create a modern living environment combining residential spaces, commercial infrastructure and lifestyle facilities.

The residential development offers thoughtfully designed 2, 2.5, 3 and 3.5 BHK premium apartments, with landscaped open spaces, recreational facilities and lifestyle amenities.

The Developer

A legacy of building excellence.

The Embassy Group is a leading Indian real estate developer and conglomerate founded in 1993, headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka.

Led by Chairman and Managing Director Jitu Virwani, the group has developed and managed over 100 million square feet of space across commercial, residential, hospitality, industrial, and educational sectors.
