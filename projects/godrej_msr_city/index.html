The Project

A new perspective on integrated living.

Godrej MSR City is a residential township by Godrej Properties, located at Shettigere, Devanahalli, North Bengaluru. Phase 2 brings thoughtfully planned 2 & 3 BHK residences to this growing airport corridor.

Phase 2 spans approximately 14.5 acres within a 62-acre township and offers 800+ homes, pedestrian-friendly landscaped spaces and a vehicle-free podium.

Residents can explore 100+ lifestyle amenities and a main clubhouse of approximately 52,000 sq. ft.

The Developer

A vision for better living.

Godrej Properties is part of the Godrej Group, whose legacy dates back to 1897. Established in 1990, Godrej Properties develops residential communities and integrated townships across India.

Godrej MSR City Phase 2 brings together 2 & 3 BHK homes, landscaped open spaces and a wide range of indoor and outdoor lifestyle amenities.
