The Project

A new perspective on integrated living.

SOBHA Hennur is a residential township by SOBHA Developers, located at Hennur, North Bengaluru. SOBHA Hennur brings thoughtfully planned 1, 2, 3 & 4 BHK residences to this growing airport corridor.

The approximately 50-acre township is planned with around 4,000 residential units and landscaped community spaces.

Residents can explore lifestyle amenities and a main clubhouse of approximately 38,000 sq. ft.
