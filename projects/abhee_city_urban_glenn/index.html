The Project

A new perspective on integrated living.

ABHEE City Urban Glenn by ABHEE Ventures Private Limited is a premium apartment township on Varthur–Whitefield Sarjapur Main Road.

Planned across 45+ acres, the township comprises approximately 3,000 apartments across 14 towers.

Choose from 2 to 4.5 BHK homes, with approximate sizes from 1,185 to 2,570 sq. ft., within a 45+ acre Scottish-themed township.
