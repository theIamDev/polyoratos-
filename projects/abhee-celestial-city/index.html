The Project

A new perspective on integrated living.

ABHEE Celestial City by Abhee Ventures is a ready-to-move residential community located at Gunjur, Varthur–Sarjapur Road, East Bengaluru.

Spread across approximately 5 acres, the project features 3 residential towers with around 397 apartments and thoughtfully planned lifestyle spaces.

Choose from 2 & 3 BHK homes, with approximately 73% open space, a 25,000 sq. ft. clubhouse and 35+ lifestyle amenities.
